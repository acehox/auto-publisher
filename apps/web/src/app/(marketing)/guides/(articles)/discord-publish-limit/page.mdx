## Can a Discord channel publish more than 10 messages an hour?

Not at once. Discord lets each announcement channel publish 10 messages per hour, and pressing
**Publish** past that shows an error instead of sending the message to the servers that follow the
channel
([Discord: Announcement Channel FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032008192-Announcement-Channel-FAQ)).

Bots get the same limit. A bot publishes through Discord's API, where publishing is called a
crosspost, and no bot can raise it. What a bot can do is keep the messages past the limit and publish
them once it resets, so a busy hour doesn't leave followers with gaps.

## How do I publish messages past the limit?

Auto Publisher ([auto-publisher.gg](/)) can hold the messages past the limit and publish them once
it resets. It comes with Premium and takes two steps:

1. **Add Auto Publisher** to your server and enable the channel, as described in
   [How to auto-publish announcement channel messages](/guides/auto-publish-discord-announcements).

   <GuideInviteButton className="my-4" />

2. **Get [Premium](/premium)** for that server.

That's all. From then on, a message posted past the limit waits instead of staying unpublished.
When Discord's limit resets, Auto Publisher publishes the waiting messages one at a time, oldest
first, so followers get them in the order you posted them. If more than 10 are waiting, the rest go
out after the next reset.

A message still waiting 24 hours after it was posted is not published, and nothing past the limit is
guaranteed to go out, because Discord decides when the channel may publish again. If you delete a
waiting message, it is not published later.

## What happens without Premium?

On the Free plan, the 11th message in an hour and every one after it stay in your server,
unpublished. Without a bot, the same messages wait until someone presses **Publish** on each of them
after the limit resets.

## When does the limit reset?

Discord doesn't publish a fixed reset time. When a channel reaches the limit, Discord tells the bot
how long to wait before that channel can publish again, and Auto Publisher waits exactly that long.
Other channels keep publishing in the meantime.

The limit is counted per channel, not per server: two announcement channels can publish 10 messages
each in the same hour. Only published messages count. A message posted but never published uses none
of it.

## How do I use less of the limit?

- **Publish only what followers need.** [Premium](/premium) filters let a channel publish only the
  messages you choose, so feed posts or chat never use up the limit. See
  [How to auto-publish only some messages](/guides/filter-which-announcements-publish).
- **Split the channel.** Each announcement channel has its own limit, so a busy feed can move to a
  channel of its own. Following servers then have to follow the new channel as well.

To check that nothing else is stopping a channel, see
[Why isn't my Discord announcement being published?](/guides/announcement-not-publishing). For how
Publish and Follow work, see [Discord crossposting explained](/guides/discord-crossposting-explained).
