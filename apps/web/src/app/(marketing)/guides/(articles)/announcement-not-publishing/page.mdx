## Where do I start?

Discord never publishes a message on its own: it reaches following servers only once someone, or a
bot, presses **Publish**.

**No bot publishing for you yet?** Auto Publisher ([auto-publisher.gg](/)) publishes every message
posted in the channels you choose. Setup is covered in
[How to auto-publish announcement channel messages](/guides/auto-publish-discord-announcements).

<GuideInviteButton className="my-4" />

**Already using Auto Publisher?** Run `/ap overview` in your server. It lists every enabled channel,
says which ones are not publishing and names the permissions they are missing. The
[dashboard](/dashboard) shows the same, with a **Fix** button on each broken channel. Then go
through the checks below in order.

## Is the channel enabled?

Auto Publisher only publishes in channels you enable. Run `/ap enable` and pick the channel, or
switch it on under **Channels** in the dashboard. A channel you never enabled is left alone.

## Does the bot have its three permissions?

The bot needs three permissions in the channel itself: {Copy.permissions.names.join(', ')}. Manage Messages is the one most often missing: Discord lets members publish their own
messages with Send Messages, but publishing anyone else's needs Manage Messages
([Discord: Announcement Channel FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032008192-Announcement-Channel-FAQ)).
A channel or category permission that denies one of them overrides the role's server-wide
permissions, so check the channel's own Permissions tab:

## Is the channel paused?

A paused channel keeps its setup but doesn't publish. `/ap overview` and the dashboard list paused
channels and say why. There are two causes, both on the Free plan:

- **Over the channel cap.** The Free plan publishes {config.limits.freeChannelsPerGuild} channels per
  server. Turn another channel off and enable this one again, or get [Premium](/premium) for
  unlimited channels.
- **It has a filter.** Filters are a Premium feature, so a filtered channel is paused on the Free
  plan rather than publishing everything. Get Premium, or remove the filter and enable the channel
  again.

## Did a filter skip it?

On Premium, a channel with a filter only publishes messages that match its rule. Open the rule with
`/ap filters` or the dashboard's **Filters** tab and check the message against each condition. See
[How to auto-publish only some messages](/guides/filter-which-announcements-publish).

## Has the channel hit Discord's hourly limit?

Discord lets each channel publish 10 messages per hour. On the Free plan, messages past it are not
published. On Premium, they are published once the limit resets. See
[How to publish messages past Discord's limit](/guides/discord-publish-limit).

## Is it a message that can't be published?

Auto Publisher skips three kinds of message, because Discord doesn't allow publishing (crossposting)
them or they are already out:

- **System messages**, such as pin notices.
- **Messages already published**, by a member or another bot.
- **Messages that arrived from a channel your server follows.** They were published at their source
  and can't be published again.

## Was it posted before the channel was enabled?

Auto Publisher publishes messages as they are posted. A message posted before the channel was
enabled, before the permissions were fixed or while the bot was offline is not published later.
Press **Publish** on it by hand.

## Is it a link waiting for its preview?

A message with a link that has no preview yet is held 5 seconds so followers get the preview. It is
published after that.

## Still not publishing?

Ask in the <a href={links.discordSupportServer}>support server</a> with the channel and roughly when the
message was posted. For how Publish and Follow work on Discord's side, see
[Discord crossposting explained](/guides/discord-crossposting-explained).
