## What is an announcement channel?

An announcement channel is a Discord text channel, marked with a megaphone icon, that other servers
can **Follow**. A message posted there is only seen in your server at first. When someone presses
**Publish** on it, Discord sends it to every server that follows the channel, where it appears as a
regular message
([Discord: Announcement Channel FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032008192-Announcement-Channel-FAQ)).

## What is crossposting?

Crossposting is the same thing as publishing. **Publish** is the button's name in Discord, and
*crosspost* is the name Discord's API gives the same action, so bots and developer tools say
"crosspost" where the app says "Publish". A crossposted message and a published message are the same
message.

Once a message is published:

- **Edits** you make to it show up in the following servers too.
- **Deleting** it removes it there as well, leaving an "[Original Message Deleted]" note.
- **@everyone and @here** mentions, and other mentions like them, are not passed on.

Discord lets each channel publish 10 messages per hour. See
[How to publish messages past Discord's limit](/guides/discord-publish-limit) for what happens
past it.

## How does another server follow an announcement channel?

1. Open the announcement channel in the server you want updates from and press **Follow**.
2. Pick your server and the channel the posts should go to. Only servers and channels where you have
   the **Manage Webhooks** permission are listed.
3. Press **Follow**.

From then on, each published message arrives in the channel you picked, posted by a bot named after
the server it came from. The channel you pick can't be an announcement channel itself. To stop,
open **Server Settings → Integrations → Channels Followed** and delete that server's entry
([Discord: Channel Following FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028384531-Channel-Following-FAQ)).

## Who can press Publish?

Members with **Send Messages** in the channel can publish their own messages. Members with **Manage
Messages** can publish anyone's. Discord's own advice is to give these only to trusted members.

## Why do messages from bots and webhooks stay unpublished?

Discord never publishes a message on its own. Every message, whoever posted it, waits until someone
presses **Publish**. When a bot, a webhook, or an RSS or news feed posts the message, nobody is
there to press it, so the following servers don't get it unless the bot that posted it also
publishes it, or a moderator does it by hand.

That is what Auto Publisher ([auto-publisher.gg](/)) is for. It presses **Publish** on every message
posted in the announcement channels you enable, from members, bots, webhooks and feeds alike, and is free for up to
{config.limits.freeChannelsPerGuild} channels per server.

## How do I create an announcement channel?

Discord offers announcement channels only to servers with Community turned on.

**Turn on Community** (once per server):

1. Open **Server Settings** from the menu under your server's name.
2. Select **Enable Community**, then **Get Started**.
3. Go through the steps. Discord requires a verification level, the explicit media content filter,
   a rules channel and a channel for Discord's community updates.
4. Agree to the guidelines and press **Finish Setup**
   ([Discord: Enabling Your Community Server](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851-Enabling-Your-Community-Server)).

**Create a new announcement channel:** press the **+** next to a channel category, choose
**Announcement** as the channel type, name it and press **Create Channel**.

**Or convert an existing text channel:** open **Edit Channel**, switch on **Announcement Channel**
and press **Save Changes**.

Post a message in it and the **Publish** option appears on it. For every command and permission
Auto Publisher uses, see [How It Works](/how-it-works).
