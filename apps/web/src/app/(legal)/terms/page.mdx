### 3.1 Technical requirements

the person configuring the Service is an Administrator, and the Bot holds the **View Channel**,
**Send Messages** and **Manage Messages** permissions in each Enabled Channel. Publishing is subject

- **Discord permits no more than 10 messages per channel to be Published in any hour.** Further

  from Servers with Premium take priority over those from Servers on the Free Plan, and a limited
  number of the first messages Published after the Bot is added to a Server, on either plan, take

  Publishing it by five seconds so that the preview can be generated.
