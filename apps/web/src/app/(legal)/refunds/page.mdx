## 1. If you are a consumer: your 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are an individual acting outside your trade, business, craft or profession, you have a
statutory right to withdraw from your subscription **within 14 days**, without giving any reason.

The period runs for 14 days from the day the contract was concluded — that is, the day your
subscription started. If your subscription began with a free trial, it was concluded on the day the
**trial** started, not on the day the first payment was taken.

**Renewing does not start a new 14-day period,** and neither does a free trial converting to a paid
subscription. The right applies once, to the contract you concluded. If you later move to a different
plan or a different billing interval, that is treated as a new contract and a new 14-day period
begins.

### Free trials

Your first subscription for a Discord server includes a **14-day free trial**, and you are not
charged during it. **At the end of the trial, payment is required**: the subscription continues at
the price shown at checkout and renews each billing period until you cancel. The trial is available
once per server.

The trial and the withdrawal period are both 14 days and begin on the same day, so they end together.
If you withdraw during the trial, no payment has been taken and **there is nothing to refund** —
withdrawing ends the contract before any charge falls due.

### How to withdraw

The quickest way is the **withdrawal button on the subscription page of your dashboard**. It is beside
your other billing controls. It asks you for one thing — an email address to send your confirmation
to — and then you press **Confirm withdrawal**. Nothing else is asked of you: no reason, no survey,
no phone call. We email you a confirmation of receipt straight away, and that email is your record.

You can also withdraw any other way you like, as long as it is clear and it reaches us before the 14
days are up:

- Email <SupportEmail /> — one line is sufficient; or
- Use the model form in section 5 below.

You do not have to use the form or the button, and you do not have to explain why. Sending your
message before the deadline is enough — it does not matter when we receive it.

### What you get back

**You get back the full amount you have paid.** We do not deduct anything for the time you had the
service, even if the bot published for you the whole time. If you withdraw during the free trial,
nothing has been charged, so there is nothing to return.

We will reimburse you without undue delay, and in any event **within 14 days** of being told you are
withdrawing. The refund is made by Paddle using the same payment method you used, at no cost to you.

The law does allow a trader to keep a proportionate amount for service already supplied — but only
where the consumer expressly asked for performance to begin during the withdrawal period, as a
separate and deliberate request. We do not ask you for that, so we do not make that deduction.

### What we do not claim

Some digital products lose the withdrawal right once the customer starts using them. **We do not
claim that.** Auto Publisher is supplied as an ongoing service rather than as a one-off download, so
starting to use it does **not** cost you your withdrawal right, and we do not ask you to waive it.

If you want to withdraw within 14 days, you can — whether or not the bot has already published
anything for you.

## 2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling is not the same as withdrawing.

- **Withdrawal** is the statutory right described above: it applies in the first 14 days and gets you
  a full refund.
- **Cancellation** can be done at any time. It stops the subscription renewing. The paid plan
  stays available until the end of the period you have already paid for, and that period is **not**
  refunded.

Cancelling during a free trial stops the subscription before any payment falls due — you keep the
paid features until the trial ends and are never charged.

If you are the subscriber, the subscription page in your dashboard links you through to Paddle's
customer portal, where you can cancel. You can always cancel by emailing <SupportEmail /> instead,
and we will arrange it — use that route if the link is not there.

When a paid subscription ends, the server returns to the free plan. The bot stays in your server and
your channel configuration and publishing rules are kept in full — nothing needs setting up again.

The free plan's limits apply again, so some channels stop publishing: any above the free limit of
three, and any that use publishing rules, because rules are a paid feature and we pause those
channels rather than publish messages you chose to filter out. Paused channels are shown as such in
your dashboard and resume exactly as configured if you subscribe again. Nothing is deleted.

## 3. Refunds outside the withdrawal period

Beyond the 14-day withdrawal period, and for business customers, refunds are not automatic — but we
are not going to hide behind that. If the service did not work as described, tell us. Where a fault
is on our side and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a price reduction
or to end the contract, and we will arrange the appropriate refund through Paddle.

Consumers additionally have the conformity rights described in the [Terms of Service](/terms),
section 4. Those rights are free of charge and are not limited by this page.

## 4. If you are a business

The right of withdrawal is a consumer right and does not apply to business customers. You can cancel
at any time as described in section 2. Refunds outside that are at our discretion, except where the
service failed to conform to what we agreed.

### Pravo na jednostrani raskid ugovora

Ako ste potrošač — fizička osoba koja sklapa ugovor izvan svoje trgovačke, poslovne, obrtničke ili
profesionalne djelatnosti — imate pravo, ne navodeći razloge za to, jednostrano raskinuti ugovor u
roku od **14 dana**.

Rok od 14 dana počinje teći od dana sklapanja ugovora, odnosno od dana kada je Vaša pretplata
započela. Ako je Vaša pretplata započela besplatnim probnim razdobljem, ugovor je sklopljen na dan
početka **probnog razdoblja**, a ne na dan naplate prvog iznosa.

**Obnova pretplate ne pokreće novi rok od 14 dana**, kao ni prelazak s besplatnog probnog razdoblja
na plaćenu pretplatu. Pravo se ostvaruje jednom, u odnosu na sklopljeni ugovor. Ako kasnije prijeđete
na drugi paket ili na drugo razdoblje naplate, to se smatra novim ugovorom pa počinje teći novi rok
od 14 dana.

Vaša prva pretplata za pojedini Discord server uključuje **besplatno probno razdoblje od 14 dana**,
tijekom kojeg Vam se ništa ne naplaćuje. **Po isteku probnog razdoblja naplata je obvezna**:
pretplata se nastavlja po cijeni prikazanoj pri kupnji i obnavlja se svakim obračunskim razdobljem
dok je ne otkažete. Probno razdoblje i rok za raskid traju 14 dana i počinju istog dana, pa istječu
zajedno.

### Kako raskinuti ugovor

**Na stranici pretplate na Vašoj nadzornoj ploči nalazi se funkcija za jednostrani raskid ugovora**,
smještena uz ostale postavke naplate. Traži od Vas samo adresu elektroničke pošte na koju ćemo poslati
potvrdu, nakon čega pritisnete gumb za potvrdu raskida. Ništa se drugo od Vas ne traži: ni razlog, ni
anketa, ni telefonski poziv. Potvrdu o primitku Vaše izjave šaljemo Vam bez odgađanja elektroničkom
poštom i ta je potvrda Vaš dokaz.

Ugovor možete raskinuti i na svaki drugi način, pod uvjetom da je izjava nedvosmislena i da nam je
pošaljete prije isteka roka od 14 dana:

- elektroničkom poštom na <SupportEmail /> — dovoljna je jedna rečenica; ili
- korištenjem obrasca za jednostrani raskid ugovora iz članka 81. stavka 1. Zakona o zaštiti
  potrošača, koji je naveden niže.

Niste dužni koristiti obrazac ni navedenu funkciju i niste dužni obrazložiti svoju odluku. Dovoljno
je da izjavu pošaljete prije isteka roka; nije važno kada je zaprimimo.

### Povrat plaćenog iznosa

**Vraćamo Vam cjelokupni plaćeni iznos.** Ne odbijamo razmjeran iznos za vrijeme u kojem ste se
uslugom koristili, čak i ako je bot cijelo to vrijeme objavljivao za Vas. Ako ugovor raskinete
tijekom besplatnog probnog razdoblja, ništa nije naplaćeno pa nema ni povrata.

Povrat izvršavamo bez odgađanja, a najkasnije u roku od **14 dana** od dana kada ste nas obavijestili
o raskidu. Povrat provodi Paddle istim sredstvom plaćanja kojim ste se koristili, bez ikakvih
troškova za Vas.
