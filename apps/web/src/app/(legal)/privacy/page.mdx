# Privacy Policy

<DocumentStatus document="/privacy" />

This policy explains what personal data Auto Publisher handles, why, how long it is kept, and what
you can do about it.

**The controller** is {entityNameWithTranslation}, whose full registered particulars are in the
[Legal Notice](/legal). For anything in this policy, write to <SupportEmail />. We do not have a Data
Protection Officer, and are not required to appoint one; that address is our privacy contact.

## 1. The short version

- **We do not store the content of your messages.** The bot reads a message to decide whether to
  publish it, in memory, and discards it. Nothing about the message text is written to a database, to
  a log, or sent anywhere else.
- **We do not ask Discord for your email address.** The only way we ever hold one is if you
  type it in yourself, and there is exactly one place that happens: the withdrawal form in
  section 7. It is used to send you one message and nothing else.
- **We never see your card details.** Payment happens entirely inside Paddle's checkout.
- **We do not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.** Discord's developer policy
  prohibits it and we do not do it.
- **We do not profile you, advertise to you, or sell your data.** There is no analytics, tracking or
  advertising code on this website at all.
- **There is no cookie banner** because we do not set any cookie that requires consent. Section 11
  lists every cookie anyway.

## 2. Who this policy is for

Two different groups of people appear in our systems, and the law treats them differently:

- **Server administrators** — you sign in to the dashboard, add the bot, and configure it. We get
  your data from you.
- **Server members** — you post in a channel the bot watches, or an administrator names you in a
  publishing rule. We did not get your data from you, so section 6 is addressed to you specifically
  and tells you where it came from.

When you sign in with Discord we ask Discord for two permissions only: `identify` (who you are) and
`guilds` (which servers you are in). **We do not request the `email` permission**, so Discord does
not give us your email address.

to, you can still use the bot through its in-Discord commands, and simply not sign in.

## 4. Where we rely on legitimate interests

Where the table above says "legitimate interests", the specific interests are: keeping the service
available and responsive by caching instead of re-querying Discord on every request; protecting the
service from abuse and overload; and keeping the service working during a Discord outage. We have
considered your interests and concluded these uses are limited, short-lived and unsurprising.

**You have the right to object to this processing at any time.** Section 8 explains how, and this
right is set out separately there because the law requires it to be brought to your attention
explicitly.

### Publishing rules can contain other people's identifiers

This is the part worth reading twice. A publishing rule has four kinds of condition, and two of them
store identifiers of people:

- **Content** — words or phrases you type. Free text, so whatever you put there is stored. Please do
  not put personal details in it.
- **Author** — **Discord user IDs**. Stored as given.
- **Mentions** — **Discord role IDs and Discord user IDs**, in one list.
- **Webhook** — Discord webhook IDs only. Not webhook names, and never webhook URLs or tokens.

If you add someone's user ID to a rule, you are asking us to store it, and we store it until you
remove it. These rules are also copied into a fast in-memory store the bot reads on every message;
that copy has no expiry and is deleted when you delete the rule or the channel.

## 6. If you are a server member

You may never have used our dashboard, and still appear in our systems. Here is the whole of it.

**Your messages.** When you post in an announcement channel where an administrator has enabled the
bot, the bot reads the message to decide whether to publish it. It reads the message text, the text
of any embeds, and the text of any interactive components, and it looks at your user ID, any roles you
mention, and whether the message came from a webhook. **All of this happens in memory and is
discarded immediately.** No message content is stored, logged, or sent to anyone. What leaves our bot
is only two numbers — the channel ID and the message ID — sent to our own publishing service so it
can ask Discord to publish that message. This is the same information Discord already has.

Those two numbers do outlive the message read, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise: the
publishing job is written to a queue so it can be retried if Discord is briefly unavailable, and that
job record is kept **{retention.queueJobs}**. It holds nothing but the channel ID, the message ID and
whether the attempt succeeded.

- **Source of the data:** Discord, through its real-time gateway, under the Message Content
  permission our bot holds.
- **Categories of data:** message text, your user ID, role mentions, webhook origin.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f)) — the server administrator has asked us to
  publish announcements from that channel, and reading the message is the only way to apply their
  rules. We keep none of it.

**Your identifiers inside a rule.** An administrator of a server you are in may have put your Discord
user ID or one of your roles into a publishing rule, as described in section 5.

- **Source of the data:** the server administrator who created the rule, not you.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f)) — operating the service the administrator has
  configured.
- **Kept for:** as long as the rule exists. You can ask us to remove it — see section 8 — and you can
  also ask the server's administrators directly, since they control the rule.

We do not build a profile of you, we do not track you across servers, and we do not use anything we
read for any purpose other than deciding whether to publish that one message.

## 7. Paid subscriptions

Payment is handled by **Paddle**, which is the merchant of record and the seller of record. Paddle
runs the checkout, takes the payment and issues the invoice. **Your name, email, billing address, tax
number and card details are entered inside Paddle's checkout and are never sent to us.**

What we send to Paddle when you start a checkout: the **Discord server ID** and the **Discord user ID**
of the person buying, so the subscription can be attached to the right server when Paddle tells us
it succeeded.

What we keep when Paddle tells us about a subscription:

| What | Legal basis | Kept for |

| Paddle customer ID and Paddle subscription ID | Contract (Art 6(1)(b)) | {retention.billingRecords} |
| The subscriber's Discord user ID | Contract (Art 6(1)(b)) | {retention.subscriberIdentifier} |

Paddle's messages to us also contain buyer details — name, email, address, tax identifier, payment
method. **We do not read or store any of them.** Our code takes only the fields in the table above and
discards the rest.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of your data (access);
- **correct** it, if it is wrong;
- **delete** it (erasure);
- **restrict** how we use it;
- **give it to you in a portable format** — including your channel configuration and publishing
  rules, which we will send you in a machine-readable file on request; and
- **stop relying on legitimate interests** — see the separate note below.

> ### Your right to object
>
> Where we rely on legitimate interests — the caching, rate-limiting and message-reading described in
> sections 3, 4 and 6 — **you have the right to object to that processing at any time**, and we must
> stop unless we can show compelling grounds that override your interests. Write to <SupportEmail />
> with "objection" in the subject line. This is a distinct right and we are setting it out separately
> so it does not get lost among the others.

Write to <SupportEmail /> to exercise any of these. We will respond within one month. There is no
charge.

Some limits, stated plainly:

- **Data held by Discord is not ours to delete.** Your account, your messages and your servers live
  with Discord. Ask Discord.
- **Data held by Paddle is not ours to delete either.** Paddle has no deletion API and is a separate
  controller for the buyer information it holds. Send erasure requests about billing data to
  `help@paddle.com`, and we will help if you ask.
- Where a rule created by a server administrator contains your identifier, we can remove it, and so
  can they.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP) — full
address in the [Legal Notice](/legal) — or to the supervisory authority where you live or work. We
would rather you told us first.

## 10. International transfers

Some of these recipients are outside the European Economic Area.

- **Discord.** If you are in the European Economic Area, your controller is Discord Netherlands BV;
  for everyone else, including the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it is Discord, Inc. in the United
  States. Discord relies on adequacy decisions where they apply, on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework
  and its Swiss and United Kingdom counterparts, and on the European Commission's Standard
  Contractual Clauses.
- **Paddle.** Which Paddle company you buy from depends on where you are. For buyers outside the
  United States it is **Paddle.com Market Limited** in the United Kingdom, and the United Kingdom has
  an **adequacy decision** from the European Commission, so no additional safeguard is needed. For
  buyers in the United States it is **Paddle.com Inc.** in the United States, where there is no
  adequacy decision — that transfer relies on the European Commission's **Standard Contractual
  Clauses** for transfers between two controllers, adopted by Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 and
  put in place by our data-sharing agreement with Paddle. Paddle also operates companies in Ireland
  and Canada and may move data between its own companies; the same Standard Contractual Clauses cover
  those transfers. You can ask Paddle for a copy of them at `privacy@paddle.com`.
- **Our database provider.** The database itself is located in the European Union, **but that alone
  does not keep the data in Europe**: the provider's contracting entity, which acts as the data
  importer, is established outside the European Economic Area in a country with no adequacy decision.
  That transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, adopted by
  Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards relied on for any of these, and for the current identity of
any provider named here only by role — write to <SupportEmail />.

## 12. Automated decision-making

**There is none.** Publishing rules match text and identifiers mechanically, and the only outcome is
whether one Discord message gets published. Nothing produces legal effects for you, and nothing
profiles you. We do not use any of this data to train machine-learning or AI models.

## 13. Security

What we actually do:

- All connections to Discord, Paddle and our own services use encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS).
- **We do not store your Discord access token.** Only an irreversible hash of it is kept, as a cache
  label, so the token cannot be recovered from our systems.
- Message content is never written to storage or to logs.
- Caches are deliberately short-lived, with the lifetimes stated above.
- Access to the database and to internal services is restricted to the application itself.

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will report it to AZOP
within 72 hours and tell affected people where the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If you find a security problem,
please tell us at <SupportEmail /> before disclosing it publicly.

## 14. Children

Discord requires all users to be at least 13, and older where local law says so. The Service is not
directed at children. In Croatia, consent-based processing of a child's data for online services is
only valid from age 16 — we do not rely on consent for anything described in this policy, but we
mention it because it is the applicable local rule.

If you believe a child's data has reached us, write to <SupportEmail /> and we will remove it.
